About this portfolio

It is with great pleasure that I introduce myself, Beena Seepersad-Vishnu, an Information Technology teacher at Waterloo Secondary School in Trinidad and Tobago. I am currently enrolled in the Post Graduate Diploma in Education programme in the Faculty of Humanities and Education at the University of the West Indies (2026). This portfolio was developed for the Pedagogy as Process course and documents how lessons and units are planned, how assessment strategies are chosen, how technology is integrated into teaching, and how Information Technology connects with other subjects and real-life situations.

“Like a scale, my teaching is not fixed, it is continuously adjusted to create balance, fairness, and meaningful learning for all students.”

From My initial teaching philosophy
