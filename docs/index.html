About this portfolio

I am an Information Technology teacher at Waterloo Secondary School in Trinidad and Tobago. I am currently enrolled in the Postgraduate Diploma in Education programme at the University of Education (2026). This portfolio was created as part of the requirement for the Pedagogy as Process course, and it documents how I plan lessons and units, choose assessment strategies, integrate technology and connect Information Technology with other subjects and real-life situations.
