E - Transformations

Students in the form four class selected for this section are currently studying Business Education and Physical Education. Thus, integrating the curriculum is significant for them for several reasons. Students can appreciate how different disciplines contribute to achieving a common goal. They can apply skills learned from Physical Education, Business, and other areas such as Mathematics, which makes learning relevant, practical, and connected to their everyday experiences.

As a change agent I have learned that change is constant. Embracing change, adapting to new circumstances and reflecting on my practices are essential first steps in influencing others positively.
