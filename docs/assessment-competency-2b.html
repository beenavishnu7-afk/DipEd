D - Growth in Assessment Competency

Competency 2 (b): Spreadsheet assessment

A Form 4 performance assessment on spreadsheets, with its scenario, instructions and rubric.

Waterloo Secondary SchoolInformation TechnologyForm 4Spreadsheet Assessment45 minutes

1. Scenario

You have been asked by a teacher to prepare a spreadsheet showing the examination results of ten students in five subjects. The teacher needs the spreadsheet to calculate each student’s total score, percentage, and pass/fail status automatically. The teacher also wants to identify the highest and lowest average scores.

You must create a spreadsheet that accurately presents the information in a clear, professional format.

2. Specific Instructions

  1. Open Microsoft Excel.

  2. Create a worksheet named Examination Results.

  3. Start at A1 and type the following headings: Student Name, Mathematics, English, IT Science, Social Studies. (See data set below)

  4. Enter the names of the ten students and their marks in the five subjects.

  5. Add a heading in G1 named Total.

  6. Enter the formula in G2 to calculate the total score for the first student.

  7. Copy the formula down to calculate the total scores for the rest of the students G3 to G11

  8. Add a heading in H1 named Average.

  9. Enter the formula in H2 to calculate the average mark for the first student.

  10. Copy the formula down to calculate the average scores for the rest of the students H3 to H11

  11. Add a heading in I1 named Pass/Fail.

  12. Use the IF function to display "Pass" if a student scores at least 50 in every subject and "Fail" if the student scores below 50 in any subject.

  13. Identify the highest and lowest average scores.

  14. Format the spreadsheet neatly and save it as Form4_Examination_Results.
