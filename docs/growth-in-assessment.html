D - Growth in Assessment Component

This section demonstrates skills acquired in designing and using varied assessment approaches. Standards of classroom assessment are explained; assessment is examined to determine the extent to which it conforms; and improvements are suggested. Performance and traditional assessment are compared. A performance assessment is created along with rubric. Discussion is conducted on what students are to understand about the topic along with benefits and challenges of administering.

Assessment tasks are significant for my teaching because it allows me to plan assessments for and of student learning. It ensures the alignment between assessment practices and learning expectations and it informs instruction.
