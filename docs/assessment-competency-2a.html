D - Growth in Assessment Competency

Competency 2 (a)

Performance assessment compared with traditional assessment.

Word count: 305

Competency 2 (a)

Assessment involves various strategies used to measure students’ knowledge, skills, and competencies, evaluate performance and provide feedback. Educators can identify students’ strengths, their areas for improvement while promoting engagement, higher-order thinking, active participation and the ability to apply learning to real-world situations by using diverse assessment methods. Two such methods of assessment are Traditional Assessment and Performance Assessment.

Traditional assessment refers to conventional methods of testing, such as standardized tests, classroom quizzes, and end of unit exams; that rely on structured, time bound evaluations which is designed to be efficient, objective, and scalable (Jiang 2026). Whereas, Performance Assessment is designed to incorporate real-world problems and tasks, giving students opportunities to develop and apply knowledge and skills in settings that resemble authentic, real-life situations (New York State Education Department, 2023).

Performance Assessment and Traditional Assessment have several distinguishing criteria. Three such criteria are: Firstly, there is the Cognitive Level & Focus. Traditional Assessment measures what students can do at a particular time, and it targets their lower-order cognitive skills such as memorization, recall, comprehension, and factual identification. In contrast, Performance Assessment measures the students’ higher-order thinking skills which involve analysis, synthesis, evaluation, critical thinking and problem-solving. A second criteria is Task Type & Nature of Response. To measure students’ knowledge and understanding, Traditional Assessment uses structured, selected-response formats such as multiple-choice, true/false, matching, and short-answer questions whilst Performance Assessment requires students to complete tasks, projects, presentations, designs, performances, or other tangible products. Thirdly, there is Structure & Path to Solutions. Traditional Assessment is usually teacher structured and is designed to have one predetermined correct answer for each test item. Performance Assessment allows for greater flexibility by students in demonstrating their knowledge and skills, with multiple acceptable approaches or solutions that may involve critical thinking, judgment and creativity.

Educators should combine both methods for comprehensive skill evaluation.
