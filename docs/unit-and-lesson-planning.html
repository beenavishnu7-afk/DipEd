B - Unit and Lesson Planning

The focus of this section of my portfolio is to demonstrate proficiency in unit and lesson planning. This section provides an explanation of task analysis while identifying skills required to perform higher order tasks and the sequence in which these tasks should be taught and developed. It outlines the unit goals, unit objectives, student engagement, learning strategies, pedagogical approaches, assessment plan, and resources needed to guide tuition over a specified time. Five lesson plans are included in this section illustrating knowledge in purposeful planning for the unit. This section also illustrates instructional scaffolding techniques in the unit.

Spreadsheet unit and its associated tasks are significant for student learning as it develops digital literacy, strengthens mathematical skills, promotes data organization and management skills, develops analytical and presentation skills, connects learning to real life situations and prepares students for the world of work. Students learn how to use Microsoft Excel and other spreadsheet applications. They incorporate functions and formulae and use charts and graphs to present data. By analyzing data from charts students develop analytical skills. Students create budgets and conduct financial planning exposing them to real-life scenarios and preparing them for further education and employment.
