E - Transformations

Sports Day lesson plan

A Form 4 spreadsheet lesson on formulas and functions, built around a Sports Day budget.

Lesson Plan

Teacher Name:

Beena Seepersad-Vishnu

Subject:

Information Technology

Class Level:

Form 4

Unit:

Spreadsheet

Lesson:

Formula and Functions

Date:

22/10/2026

  1. Prior knowledge/skills

What are the steps to

  • Open a spreadsheet?

  • Create a worksheet?

  • Enter, edit and format data in cells?

Identify row, column and cell reference.

What is the symbol for multiplying in spreadsheet?

  1. Lesson objectives :

At the end of this lesson students should be able to:

1) Identify at least four items of equipment needed for a School Sport Day with at least 80% accuracy.

(cognitive, remember)

2) Manipulate data in a spreadsheet with at least 80% accuracy. (cognitive, remember)

3) Apply formula and function to calculate item cost and total expenses and revenue. (cognitive, apply)

4) Format a spreadsheet budget with appropriate headings, numerical entries, formulas and currency formatting. (psychomotor)

5) Collaborate respectfully, demonstrate responsible financial decision-making (Affective)

  1. Set induction

Present a scenario: Our school is preparing for its annual sports day. The principal has asked our class to help organize the event, but the school has a limited budget. How can we decide what to buy, calculate the cost and determine whether the money available will be enough. Ask students to suggest what the school might need to purchase. Ask for answers from Physical education students in class. Explain that the students will become the school sports day planning team and must use a spreadsheet to prepare the budget.

  1. Resources including digital resources

  • Laptop computers

  • Smart tv

  • Prepared worksheet with sample prices

  • Whiteboard and markers

  • Sports equipment price list

  • Assessment checklist

  1. Content/ Teaching Points

1) Present Sports Day Scenario and ask introductory questions.

2) Explain budget, revenue, expenses and profit and loss

3) Demonstrate spreadsheet formula and formatting

4) Distribute the budget template and guide students through entering data and calculating item costs

5) Assign the Sports Day budget activity

6) Circulate to provide guidance

7) Checks answers asks review questions

  1. Teaching strategies

Problem-based learning, collaboration, teacher modelling and demonstration, questioning and guided discovery, curriculum integration

Differentiated Instruction

Contingency plan

  1. Student Activities

1) Identify sporting equipment and suggest possible expenses.

2)Answer questions, observe demonstration and identify the correct formulas and functions.

3) Create the budget using Microsoft Excel by entering data, formula and functions

4) Recommend cost saving measure or increasing income method

  1. Sectional review/ Assessment

Assessment Rubric

Award points as follows:

Assessment Criterion Marks
Correct entry of at least five equipment items and related data 5
Accurate use of multiplication Formulas 5
Correct use of SUM function 5
Accurate revenue and profit/loss calculation 5
Appropriate formatting and organization 3
Sensible cost saving recommendation 2
Total 25

  1. Closure

Ask students to complete the following statement:

“Today I learnt that spreadsheets can help a school organize Sports Day by….”

Ask two students to share their responses.

Spreadsheets are useful tools for organizing data, calculating costs, analyzing financial information and making decisions.
